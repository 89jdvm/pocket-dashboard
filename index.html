The short answer

Five things the data says, strongest first.

  1. Project management at software and AI companies is your best balance of market and fit. You meet about 75-80% of the usual requirements. In software, 45% of these jobs are open to someone in Ecuador and 91% are worked in English or Spanish. The catch: it is small (41 ads and 38 employers open to Ecuador in 60 days), and about a third of the ads want software-team methods and tools (Agile, Jira) you don't have on record yet.
  2. Sales and business development for software is the biggest market, but you don't fit it yet. It has 94 employers with a job open to Ecuador, far more than any other pocket. You meet only 44% of the real requirements; the ads ask for CRM tools, sales quotas and SaaS sales experience. Pay splits in two: software companies publish a median of $10,300 a month, agencies $1,450.
  3. AI data work is the fastest way in, not a career. Half of these jobs are open to Ecuador, but 160 of 191 are freelance gigs with no guaranteed hours, and two companies posted 79% of them. Listed rates run from $6 to $100 an hour, set partly by where you live.
  4. "AI + sustainability" fails the test as a remote job from Ecuador. Sustainability and ESG jobs are 10 of the 4,483 remote jobs open to you. Climate and carbon services: 0 of 45. You fit sustainability best (90%), but those jobs are on-site in Europe. That makes it a route for the move, not for income now.
  5. Treat this ranking as provisional. 72% of the remote jobs open to Ecuador come from one tech-heavy job board, and consulting contracts and tenders are not ranked yet. Both checks run before you pick on 30 Sep.

Pay and visas: the two bars

Bar 1: USD 2,000/month keeps the family going. Bar 2: about EUR 4,000/month is what a digital-nomad visa for a family of three needs.

What remote jobs hiring from Latin America pay

Median monthly pay by job area, from 211 remote ads on Get on Board (a Latin American job board that publishes pay), Oct 2025 to Sep 2026.

What that means

Only 4% of the job ads publish pay (617 of 15,051). Most that do are US-based companies, so the high pay shares in the pocket table are optimistic.

Remote jobs hiring from Latin America pay less. For non-engineering ads the median starting pay is USD 1,600/month. 41% clear USD 2,000; 2 of 93 clear EUR 4,000.

Seniority matters a lot: senior ads have a median of USD 3,500 (90% clear USD 2,000). So target senior titles, and employers that pay US or EU rates, not Latin American rates.
